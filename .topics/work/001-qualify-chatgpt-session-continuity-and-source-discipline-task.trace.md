## Objective

Establish the OpenAI/ChatGPT-specific host adaptation for session survivability, carried-source preference, connector boundaries, and human interaction without tainting Core or portable Native process semantics.

## Done Criteria

- `interop-openai` owns a small registered `.topics/.processes` discovery surface and one ChatGPT-specific continuity/source-discipline process.
- ChatGPT volatile runtime state is not treated as durable project authority; meaningful risk-sensitive checkpoints are returned on a durable host transport surface.
- Qualified carried Workspace/package material is preferred before immutable recovery and live connector access.
- GitHub connector read/search is recovery/source access, not a replacement for carried material or implicit semantic authority.
- Connector/remote mutation requires separate explicit authorization and does not follow from `grounded-to-act`.
- Human messages/attachments remain input/transport/evidence according to their real role and do not become hidden durable authority by accident.
- The process can be selected or omitted with the OpenAI target; Core behavior remains host-neutral.

- No new schema or dot-prefixed structural namespace is invented.
