# ChatGPT Operator Status And Emergency Recovery Presentation Evidence

## Supported Claim Or Question

- Supported Claim Or Question: whether ChatGPT Web can present ordinary checkpoint state and broken-sandbox recovery as a stable, grounded operator status surface without turning presentation into Tiinex authority.
- Evidence Role: host-presentation verification for checkpoint ergonomics and emergency recovery grounding.
- Target Artifact: ChatGPT Session Continuity And Source Discipline.
- Review Context: Sigma found the compact status box highly legible and requested a stable legend, package-link adjacency at the bottom of checkpoint messages, and an explicit emergency recovery mode known from early ChatGPT Web grounding.

## Provenance

- Known Source: Sigma human UX feedback in the active ChatGPT Web session, the shared Business checkpoint transition, the ChatGPT broken-sandbox recovery transition, and the ChatGPT Web Target Entry.
- Preservation Basis: presentation rules are encoded in durable Tiinex Process/Target material and continuity integrity is resealed after maintenance.
- Provenance Limits: UI rendering details remain host-specific observations; this evidence does not claim undocumented ChatGPT retention guarantees.

## Evidence Material

- Material: shared status legend, ChatGPT-specific checkpoint placement, emergency recovery status contract, and Target Entry grounding primer.
- Material Kind: process/host-presentation verification.
- Shared Legend: `✅` means yes/recommended/required when stated; `◯` means optional/not necessary; `❌` means no/do not; `⚠️` means attention/degraded or emergency state.
- Legend Boundary: icons are presentation only; field wording supplies the exact disposition and authority remains in qualified Tiinex material.
- Normal Checkpoint Ergonomics: ChatGPT Web guidance places deeper explanation above, the compact actionable status projection near the bottom, and the exact checkpoint/Handoff Package link immediately below the status projection.
- Branch Title Presentation: suggested ChatGPT conversational dimensions use spaced separators such as `1 - 1 - 1 (Recovery Checkpoint)` without changing Tiinex carrier dimensions.
- Emergency Header: broken/unreliable runtime presentation uses `EMERGENCY RECOVERY ⚠️` rather than calling every sandbox failure a Tiinex Handoff.
- Emergency Disposition: stop continued mutation in the unreliable runtime, require a fresh chat for conservative recovery, identify the exact latest safe checkpoint, re-upload the exact package, reuse saved transport text or regenerate it externally, and apply any recovery delta only after bootstrap/grounding.
- Target Grounding: ChatGPT Web Target Entry now makes the status legend and broken-sandbox recovery transition discoverable/relevant during target grounding while explicitly preserving independent Process applicability and authority.
- Placement Boundary: package link adjacency and bottom placement are ChatGPT host ergonomics, not portable Core semantics.

## Preservation And Fidelity

- Preservation State: Business and Interop OpenAI process lineages plus ChatGPT Target Entry are integrity-verified after maintenance.
- Fidelity Notes: the design preserves the exact distinction between checkpoint/recovery presentation and Handoff/work-transfer authority.
- Known Losses: no automatic UI widget is required; current presentation is grounded behavior for the recipient/host adapter.

## Interpretation Limits

- Not Yet Used As: platform guarantee, automatic sandbox-health detector, Handoff routing, recipient authority, acceptance, current-work selection, or remote mutation authority.
- Does Not Prove: that every branch loses runtime state, that every slow page is a broken sandbox, or that every checkpoint requires a fresh chat.
- Must Not Be Treated As: permission to reconstruct missing authority from chat chronology or to replace exact package transport with host-authored semantic summaries.
- Need For Review: future host dogfood may refine field wording or presentation while keeping the grounded legend and authority boundary stable.
